## Coverage notes
- AC1–AC11 are each covered by at least one test case.
- Out-of-scope items explicitly not covered: public catalogue browsing (US-011, not in this scope pass), an in-app QR scanner (AS-012.1), archiving completed entries (DEC-40), and cross-device persistence of the joined-quizzes list (DEC-41).
- The tutor-side share management perspective (link creation, rotation, participant list) is covered separately in [TC-US-010-share-quiz-via-link-or-qr.md](TC-US-010-share-quiz-via-link-or-qr.md) to avoid duplication.
- Playing the quiz itself (question interaction, scoring, media playback) is covered in [TC-US-013-play-quiz-and-submit-attempt.md](TC-US-013-play-quiz-and-submit-attempt.md); this file focuses on the join/list lifecycle only.

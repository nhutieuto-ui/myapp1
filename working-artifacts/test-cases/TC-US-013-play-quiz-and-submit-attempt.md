## Coverage notes
- AC1–AC13 are each covered by at least one test case; one additional case (TC_PLAY_014) targets the server-side scoring security NFR explicitly called out in the story.
- Flashcard review is explicitly out of scope for this story and is covered separately in [TC-US-014-review-flashcard-set.md](TC-US-014-review-flashcard-set.md).
- TC_PLAY_005 and TC_PLAY_006 are marked Automation = No in this phase because keyboard-only drag-alternative interaction and media replay verification are better suited to manual/exploratory and accessibility-focused verification before investing in automation.
- Timers, anti-cheat, proctoring, and manual grading are out of scope per the story and are not covered here.
